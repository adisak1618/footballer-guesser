Don'ts

Boundaries are the system. These are off-limits.

✕ No Inter / Roboto / Poppins / Montserrat
✕ No purple gradients (AI slop)
✕ No mascots, illustrations, claymation
✕ No Bebas / Anton in body text
✕ No light mode as default
✕ No hover-only affordances on mobile
✕ No touch targets under 44px
✕ No body text under 16px
✕ No duplicate tag colors in one room
✕ No 3-column SaaS card grids
