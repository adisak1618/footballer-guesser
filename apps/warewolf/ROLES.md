# Warewolf — Role Reference

The curated role set for **Warewolf**, the social-deduction game in the Headball multi-game platform. 15 roles across 3 teams. Designed for ~9–15 players, party format.

Source: roles trimmed and balanced from the broader werewolf canon (see [online-station.net article](https://www.online-station.net/entertainment/690636)).

## Werewolf team (4)

Goal: eliminate the village until werewolves equal or outnumber villagers.

### Werewolf — มนุษย์หมาป่า

Each night, wake with the other werewolves and collectively choose one player to eliminate. Knows all other werewolves.

### Wolf Cub — ลูกมนุษย์หมาป่า

Aligned with the werewolves. If the Wolf Cub is eliminated, the werewolf team gets **two** kills on the following night instead of one.

### Minion — สมุนรับใช้

Knows who the werewolves are but does **not** wake with them and cannot kill. Wins with the werewolf team. Appears as a normal villager to all checks.

### Sorcerer — นางปีศาจ

Aligned with the werewolves. Each night, chooses one player to inspect; the moderator reveals whether that player is the Seer. Does not kill. Does not wake with the wolves.

---

## Village team (10)

Goal: identify and vote out every werewolf.

### Villager — ชาวบ้าน

No special ability. Wins through discussion and voting.

### Seer — เทพพยากรณ์

Each night, chooses one player. Learns whether that player is a werewolf. **Note:** the Lycan reads as a werewolf to the Seer despite being village-aligned.

### Cupid — กามเทพ

On the first night only, chooses **two** players to link as lovers. If one lover is eliminated for any reason, the other dies immediately of heartbreak. Lovers learn each other's identities on night one.

### Bodyguard — บอดี้การ์ด

Each night, chooses one player to protect. If the werewolves target that player, the kill is cancelled. Cannot protect the same player two nights in a row. May or may not protect themselves (decide at setup).

### Prince — เจ้าชาย

Immune to being voted out during the day. If the Prince receives the elimination vote, the vote is voided and the day phase ends without an execution. The Prince's role is revealed when this triggers.

### Mayor — นายกเทศมนตรี

The Mayor's vote counts as **two** votes during the day phase. May choose when to reveal the role (rules variant: forced reveal at first vote vs. optional reveal — decide at setup).

### Hunter — นายพราน

When eliminated (by werewolves at night or by vote during the day), the Hunter immediately chooses one other player who is also eliminated.

### Tough Guy — หนุ่มถึก

Survives the first werewolf attack. On the night attacked, the Tough Guy stays alive but is mortally wounded — they die at the **end of the following night** regardless of any further protection. Their condition is not publicly announced.

### Old Hag — แม่หมอ

Each day, curses one player. The cursed player cannot cast a vote and cannot be targeted by other night abilities until the next day. (Variant: the curse lasts until cleared — decide at setup.)

### Lycan — ลูกครึ่งมนุษย์หมาป่า

A villager who appears as a werewolf to the Seer. Wins with the village. Has no other ability — the role exists to mislead and protect the real werewolves.

## Neutral (1)

Independent victory condition; not aligned with either team.

### Tanner — ยาจก

Wants to die. **Wins immediately** if voted out by the village during the day phase. If killed at night by werewolves, the Tanner does not win — they simply die. If the Tanner wins, the game ends and no other team wins.
